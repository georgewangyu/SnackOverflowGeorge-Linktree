# SnackOverflowGeorge Linktree Vision

SnackOverflowGeorge Linktree should be a fast, static personal brand hub that
points visitors toward George's strongest public projects, social surfaces, and
creator channels.

## Product Thesis

The page is useful when it acts as a clean routing layer, not a mini portfolio
CMS. A visitor should immediately understand who George is, what to click next,
and how the public creator/product surfaces connect.

## Goals

- Keep the site static, responsive, accessible, and easy to update.
- Preserve the image/icon discovery workflow for low-friction content changes.
- Make the best project and social links obvious.
- Keep deployment simple enough that the page can change with the public brand.

## Non-Goals

- Do not add backend complexity unless a real conversion flow requires it.
- Do not turn the site into a long-form portfolio before the link hub is sharp.
- Do not let decorative interactions undermine accessibility or load speed.
